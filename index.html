Import Academic Records

Import your courses from Academic Records Summary:

  1. Go to SUIS → Student → Student Records → Academic Transcript and view your Academic Records Summary
  2. Save the page as HTML (Webpage, Complete) (preferred) or use your browser's Save as PDF
  3. Upload the saved HTML or PDF file below

Or alternatively, upload your YÖK Transcript PDF file (not preferred)

Choose your major and double major before importing
Mobile tip: SUrriculum is designed for desktop. On mobile, landscape works best for the Scheduler, and portrait works best for the Term Planner. Try rotating your phone if something feels cramped.
